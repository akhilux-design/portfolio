Nuvionx.

Nuvionx Ltd is a UK-based clinical engineering company that maintains, calibrates and repairs medical equipment for hospitals and healthcare centres. I designed the brand identity and a responsive website that presents its services with the precision and reliability the sector expects.

01 — Brief

Precision you can rely on.

Nuvionx services the equipment hospitals depend on: maintenance, calibration, ultrasound probe repair, bed management and asset audits. The people choosing a provider are clinical engineering teams and facilities managers, who need to see capability, coverage and compliance quickly.

The company needed a brand and a website from scratch. The site had to explain a technical service catalogue clearly, earn trust before the first call, and work equally well on a desktop in an estates office and a phone on a ward.

02 — Scope

Ten pages, three breakpoints.

Core
  • Home
  • About Us
  • Contact
Our Services
  • Medical Equipment Maintenance
  • Clinical Engineering Support
  • Asset Management & Audit Support
  • Professional Hospital Bed Management
  • Ultrasound Probe Repair
More
  • Sectors We Serve
  • Inventory & Life Cycle Management

Every page was designed at desktop, tablet and mobile, with dedicated navigation menus and service dropdowns for each size.

03 — Direction

Clinical, not cold.

G1

Trust signals up front: a persistent Precision · Reliability · Compliance bar, contact details in the header, and client testimonials on the home page.

G2

Services first: each service gets its own page with a consistent hero, benefits and enquiry form, so buyers can compare like for like.

G3

Real environments: photography of labs, wards and equipment in dark, blue-graded heroes, set against light content sections.

G4

One system, three sizes: shared components for buttons, service cards, feature cards and forms across every breakpoint.

04 — Brand Identity

A mark with a pulse.

A deep medical blue carries the brand, headers and navigation; a brighter cyan picks out calls to action and icons. Kumbh Sans sets headings with a clean, engineered feel and Hind Madurai keeps long service descriptions easy to read.

06 — Responsive

Built for the ward, too.

Tablet and mobile layouts reflow each section and replace the dropdowns with a full-screen blue menu that keeps phone, address and Book Now within reach.

07 — Prototype

In motion.

A clickable Figma prototype covered the full site: an animated logo splash, navigation and service dropdowns, and scroll-triggered reveals on headings, images and buttons, handed over as the reference for development.
