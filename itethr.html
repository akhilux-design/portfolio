01 — Overview

One place for your people.

iTethr is a community platform for international students, organisations, creators and global users. People build structured communities, offer services and connect through real-time social tools.

An in-house AI assistant, Aeono, runs through the product: in search, in chat and in the marketplace.

I designed the iOS app end to end for AeonovX SIA, a product company in Riga: product and interaction design, the design system and developer handoff.

02 — The challenge

Feeds and tab bars everywhere.

Social and community apps feel like endless feeds and tab bars. International students especially struggle to find their people, whether that’s university departments, local communities or trusted services, in one place. Institutions also lack a structured way to run many sub-communities under one roof.

G1
Make Home feel alive, not like a list
G2
Give institutions real structure
G3
Build trust between strangers

04 — Key experiences

How it works.

01

A Home without a tab bar

Floating, animated bubbles stand for communities and connections, so Home feels spatial and present. The Aeono AI search bar sits at the bottom, iFeed is top-left, and Marketplace and Notifications are top-right.

02

Spaces

Swipe between Home, Favorites, Global and custom Spaces such as “Study Room” or “Startup Notes”. Long-press any bubble for quick actions.

03

Structured communities

A community holds Hubs, and each Hub holds i/ Rooms for chat, voice, a store and a forum. Members always know where a conversation lives.

04

Community Pro & i/Communities

For institutions such as a university with departments. Board members send targeted announcements that cascade to departments, and an admin bridge connects board and department admins.

05

Aeono AI throughout

Conversational search with a choice of model, and smart reminders detected from chat, such as “meeting at 10pm”.

06

Tethr connections & trust

Connections are mutual and consent-based instead of follow and follow-back. Profiles show a Trust Score, Tethrd level and verification badges for Students, Freelancers and Professionals.

07

Marketplace

Services, rentals, events and AI tools & agents, found by category and reached by DM or external link. Payments and reviews were deliberately left out of V1.

08 — Outcome & reflection

What shipped.

  • 10 complete user flows, ~150 screens in Light and Dark
  • A scalable system: 45 components, ~300 tokens
  • A dev-ready file organised by flow, with numbered screens
L1

Spatial UI needs clear anchors. Bubbles make Home feel alive, but fixed positions for search, iFeed and notifications are what keep it usable.

L2

Structure pays off at scale. Community → Hub → i/Room gave every new feature, from Community Pro to the admin bridge, an obvious home.

L3

Theme tokens early. Binding every colour to a semantic variable made dark mode a mode switch rather than a second design.
